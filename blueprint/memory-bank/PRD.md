# MeetFlow AI - Product Requirements

## Overview
**Name:** MeetFlow AI  
**Type:** Mobile App (iOS & Android)  
**Purpose:** AI-powered meeting assistant with real-time transcription and action extraction.

## Target Users
- Consumers/General public
- Internal teams
- B2B clients

## Core Features/Pages
1. **AI Meeting Assistant Dashboard**: Central hub, record trigger, summaries, tasks.
2. **Live Meeting Recording**: Real-time waveforms, live transcription, bookmarks.
3. **AI Meeting Report & Tasks**: Post-meeting view, summaries, action items, sentiment charts.

## Data Requirements
- **Supabase**: Auth, Database (PostgreSQL), Realtime, Storage.

## Aesthetic
- **Visuals**: Purple gradient (#8B5CF6), glassmorphism, clean white/gray cards.
- **Vibe**: Cyber-Industrial/Modern.

## AI Agents
- Heavily used (Antigravity).
